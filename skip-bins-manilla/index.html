Skip hire in Manilla

Manilla is a country town known for paragliding at nearby Mt Borah and for Lake Keepit and Split Rock Dam. Skip jobs here are often older-home renovations, deceased-estate clear-outs and shed or farm clean-ups. Because it's about 40 minutes from Tamworth, ask whether delivery distance changes the price and how long the hire runs.

Manilla Landfill

Tamworth Regional Council's Manilla Landfill has limited hours. Council's page currently lists Tuesday and Friday 1pm to 4pm and Sunday 8:30am to 11:30am. Check before you go, as hours have changed in recent years.

Sizes

3–4 m³ suits a garage or bathroom, 6 m³ a kitchen, and 8–9 m³ a house or shed. See the size guide.

Council rules

Manilla is in Tamworth Regional Council. A skip on the road needs section 68 approval ($120 in 2025–26). On your own land, approval generally isn't needed. See the permit guide.
